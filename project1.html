Booz Allen Hamilton

Bridging the gap between medical device regulators and manufacturers

I was the lead researcher and designer for a regulatory interactions program and enterprise communication features that aimed to improve communication between the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and medical device manufacturers.

Note: Because this government work is confidential, I'm limited in what I can share. Reach out if you want to hear more, and I’ll share what I can.

Overview

Most of my work focuses on building communication tools that would improve real-time interactions, conversation tracking across multiple legacy applications, and long-term interaction trends. High-quality communication between FDA and device manufacturers is associated with improvements in medical devices, resulting in the U.S. market having access safer and more effective products.

Results & Impact

Because I was involved as both a researcher and designer, I was able to create designs and measure it’s impact on business strategy. Across the various projects, I created products that:

  1. Improved the user level of ease scores, improving overall usability
  2. Reduced tool-switching friction, resulting an increase in efficiency
  3. Increased potential user loyalty to the program, fulfilling long-term retention goals

Notable Highlights

Conducted interviews

I led discovery interviews with 4 users to understand key problems and get feedback about the current process of communication. Below is a quote from a real user of the system. It highlights the importance of efficient collaboration and commmunication.

“ Industry users and FDA have the same goals. We both want to get the product to market as quickly and using the least resources as possible to get the submissions reviewed and completed.” - User

Evaluated messaging prototypes

I led usability testing of a threaded messaging prototype with 10 users in order to create initial UI designs that addressed the problems.

Created enterprise communication features

I designed a consolidated interactions tracking log for multi-channel communications from multiple legacy integrations.

Designed end-to-end product UIs

I collaborated with business & developers to design a reviewer workflow and system to track long-term interactions for the full journey of a medical device to the U.S. market.
